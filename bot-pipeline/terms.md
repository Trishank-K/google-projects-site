# Bot Pipeline terms of service

_Effective 26 November 2026_

Bot Pipeline is a personal Discord bot built and run by Trishank-K. These terms apply to anyone who uses it or views what it posts.

## What the service is

Bot Pipeline uploads the operator's own finished videos to the operator's Google Drive, posts a view-only link with the video's title and thumbnail to the operator's Discord server, and later deletes the video from Google Drive. It's a private tool, not a public or commercial service, and it's offered free of charge.

## Who can use it

Only the operator signs in with Google and controls the bot. Members of the Discord server can view its posts and, if the server's admins allow it, use its slash commands. Using it means you also follow [Discord's Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Google's Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms).

## Acceptable use

Don't use the bot to share content you don't have the rights to, or anything unlawful or abusive, and don't try to disrupt it or reach data it isn't meant to handle. The operator may restrict anyone's access to the bot at any time.

## Shared links

Video links are viewable by anyone who has them, and they stop working once the video is deleted from Google Drive, which may be after 3 hours, 1 day or never, as the operator chooses. Don't rely on a link staying available, and don't re-share videos without permission.

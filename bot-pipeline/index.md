# Bot Pipeline

Bot Pipeline is a personal Discord bot. When I finish a short video, it uploads the video file to a folder in my own Google Drive, makes a view-only share link, and posts the video's title, thumbnail and link to a channel on my Discord server. After a time I choose (3 hours, 1 day, or never), it deletes the video from Google Drive again.

- **Google access:** only the `drive.file` permission. The app can see and change only the files and the folder it creates itself, and nothing else in the Drive.
- **Who uses it:** only me, on my own computer. It isn't offered to anyone else.

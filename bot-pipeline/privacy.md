# Bot Pipeline privacy policy

_Effective 26 November 2026_

Bot Pipeline is a personal Discord bot run by Trishank-K on their own computer, for their own use. This policy explains what it does with data from Google.

## What the app accesses

The app asks for one Google permission: **`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file`**. With it, the app can see and change only the files and folders it creates itself. It can't see, read, change or delete any other file in the Google Drive account.

It doesn't ask for your name, email address, contacts or any other Google data.

## How the data is used

- **Uploads:** the app creates one folder in the signed-in Google Drive and uploads video files from the operator's own computer into it.
- **Sharing:** it sets each uploaded video to "anyone with the link can view" and posts that link, with the video's title and thumbnail, to the operator's own Discord channel.
- **Deletion:** after the retention time the operator chooses (3 hours, 1 day, or never), it permanently deletes the uploaded video from Google Drive.

The app uses Google data only to provide these features.

## Storage and sharing

- The Google sign-in token and the list of uploaded file IDs are stored only on the operator's computer. They are never sent anywhere except to Google to make these requests.
- The app has no servers, analytics, advertising or tracking. It doesn't sell, rent or transfer Google user data to anyone.
- The only data leaving Google Drive is the share link, title and thumbnail posted to the operator's Discord channel, as described above.

Bot Pipeline's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

You can remove the app's access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>. Files the app already uploaded stay in Google Drive until it or you delete them.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. For questions, [open an issue](https://github.com/Trishank-K/google-projects-site/issues).
